# Code of Conduct

Hivemind follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be kind and patient. People of every age and every level of experience
  use and build this project.
- Disagree about ideas, never attack people.
- No harassment, insults, or sexual or hateful content.
- Never share someone else's private information (names, accounts,
  servers, screenshots) without their permission.
- Help newcomers: a beginner's question is a good question.

If someone breaks these rules, report it to the maintainers through the
repository (for example a private message to a maintainer). Reports are
handled in confidence. Maintainers may remove comments, issues or pull
requests, or block people who break the rules.
