# Changelog

## UI Preview

The first public download. It lets you look around the Hivemind app before
the bot itself is released.

**What it is**

- The full Hivemind window, running a built-in **demo**. Every number in it is
  made up, and a red banner across the top says so.
- **Start does nothing.** The preview cannot play the game, does not open
  Roblox and does not touch your account.

**What you can look at**

- **Macro**: gathering, the pattern studio (make, view and share gather
  patterns), collectibles, quests, bug runs, planters and the live view.
- **AI Progress**: the overview, gear goals, materials, the hive planner,
  quests, and the "Thinking" page where the AI explains each choice.
- **Boosting**: boosts, timers and the hotbar.
- **Stats**: honey per hour, trips and fields (demo numbers).
- **Settings**, the search box, and stream mode.

**Downloads**

- Windows: `Hivemind-UI-Preview.exe`, a one-file installer. It installs for
  your user only (no admin prompt) and sits beside a real Hivemind without
  touching it.
- Linux: `Hivemind-UI-Preview-x86_64.AppImage`, one file. Make it executable
  and double-click it.

**Not in this release**

- The bot itself. Test builds come later.
- macOS.
