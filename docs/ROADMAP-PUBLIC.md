# Hivemind roadmap

What Hivemind can do today, what is being worked on, and what is planned.
Want to help with something? Open an issue saying which item, or send a pull
request (see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md)). The item codes match the
project's own list, so use them when you ask.

## Built

- **Plays on its own by looking at the screen**: gathers, converts, walks home, recovers from deaths and rejoins.
- **Chooses fields by measured honey per hour**, not a fixed list.
- **Token chasing with a "token brain"**: weighs each token's value against the walk to it.
- **Quests**: reads the quest menu, works on the steps it can do, hands quests in.
- **Planters, dispensers, buying and crafting** within your settings; never spends Robux.
- **Bug runs and the Coconut Crab fight**, on their respawn timers.
- **Fireflies at night** (Moon Charms).
- **The desktop app**: live view, settings, stats, a pattern studio, a hive planner, credits.
- **Optional Telegram and Discord alerts**; F12 panic key.
- **UI Preview** download for Windows (Linux coming).

## Being tested

- **T2 · Token AI v2**: more token kinds and buff rules (Token Link, balloons, Haste, Flame...).
- **Smarter planner**: every errand and quest priced in honey, one plan.
- **Per-field experiments**: when to convert, whether to chase tokens.
- **G11 · Materials checklist**: what your next gear still needs.

## Next

- **Reliable hive claim** on a fresh server join.
- **H18 · Red hive AI**: Scorching Star tokens, flames, Precise targets (needs red-hive testers).
- **Q7 · Goo collection** (Gummy Bee), for quests that ask for goo.
- **U15 · Test builds of the real bot** for Windows and Linux.

## Planned

- **F0 · Auto-setup**: read your hive and set the pattern, token and quest priorities for you.
- **G10 · Your own progression plan**: reorder, add or remove goals; share it as a preset.
- **N2 · Choose your nectars**: which nectars, their priority and how much.
- **H4b · Wax calculator**: the odds of each beequip stat.
- **T5 · Puffshrooms**: pop them when they spawn (an option).
- **T14 · Bloom hunting**: pop Blooms and catch the petals.
- **M3 / M3b · Commando Chick, and keeping your distance from mobs.**
- **U15f · Auto update** (an option you turn on).
- **RL6j · Use your Windows PC while it plays** (researching, including Android emulators).
- **RL7 · macOS.**

Ideas from the community are credited in the app (Settings → Credits).
