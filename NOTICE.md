## Natro Macro (GPL-3.0)

Hivemind's gather patterns and field walking paths are derived from
**Natro Macro** (https://github.com/NatroTeam/NatroMacro), licensed under the
GNU General Public License v3.0. Its AutoHotkey patterns and paths were
transpiled into Hivemind's own data format. Derived material includes:

- `data/world/natro_paths.yaml`, `data/world/natro_patterns.yaml` (generated by
  `scripts/transpile_natro.py` and `scripts/transpile_natro_patterns.py`)
- the gather patterns and field paths used by `src/hivemind/act/patterns.py`,
  `src/hivemind/act/routes.py` and the field defaults in `data/world/`
- `data/templates/stickerstack/`: Natro's Sticker Stack menu bitmaps, decoded
  from the base64 PNGs in its `nm_image_assets` (see `SOURCE.txt` there)
- constants and strategies ported from Natro and cited where they are used,
  among them the night-grass colours in `src/hivemind/vision/night.py` and
  the Memory Match strategy and timings in `src/hivemind/act/memorymatch.py`

Thank you to the Natro team and contributors.

## Bee Swarm Simulator Fandom wiki (CC BY-SA 3.0)

Game facts (fields, items, bees, mobs, quests, prices) and many reference
images were taken from the Bee Swarm Simulator Fandom wiki
(https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com), whose text is licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 (CC BY-SA 3.0). Material from it
lives in:

- `data/knowledge/` (the YAML knowledge files; the wiki facts in them)
- `data/knowledge/tokens/wiki/` (token images; sources in its `SOURCES.md`;
  `BeamStorm.png` has its chat column blurred)
- `data/knowledge/gear/wiki/` (gear pictures; sources in its `SOURCES.md`)

Adapted wiki text in these files is shared under CC BY-SA 3.0. The game art
itself (bee, item, token, gear and field images) belongs to Bee Swarm
Simulator's creator, Onett. The crops of the game's and the Roblox client's
own screen in `data/templates/` belong to Onett or Roblox Corporation (the
Roblox menus, shop and respawn buttons are Roblox's). Both are included only
so the bot can recognise the screen and are not covered by this project's
licence.

The field and item icons (`data/game_icons/`) and bee icons
(`data/bee_faces/`) are not part of the public source release. They are
Onett's art, so each player downloads them from the wiki for their own
personal use by running `scripts/fetch_game_icons.py` and
`scripts/fetch_bee_faces.py` once; without them the app shows plain
placeholders.
